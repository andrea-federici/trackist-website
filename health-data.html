Washington State

Consumer health data privacy policy

Updated November 24, 2026

Andrea Federici of Tromsø, Norway, provides StrideBuddy. In this policy, “we” means Andrea Federici. This policy covers consumer health data about you under Washington’s My Health My Data Act, whether you live in Washington or your covered data is collected there.

1. What we collect and why

Your training diary can reveal your physical condition. You can record effort, how a session felt and a preset tag such as “Tired” or “Heavy”. If you write about soreness, pain, an injury or an illness in a note or session description, those details are also part of the diary. We use your entries, including workouts, dates, repetitions, weights, times, rest and race results, to keep your diary, sync it across your devices and show your progress.

We also use information you give us about your event, competition category, birth year, personal bests, training schedule and goals. With AI features enabled, we use selected profile information, diary entries and recent training summaries to suggest or change a training week, give workout feedback or estimate race performance. Depending on your event and the performance data available, these results can include an estimate of your aerobic capacity (VO₂max). When you ask the app to fill in a session, the text, voice recording or photo you supply can contain health information. The app turns on AI the first time you accept the combined terms and AI disclosure. Accepting an update preserves your existing choice. You can turn it off in Settings. Saving a diary entry still syncs its contents, including any health details you choose to write.

Our sources are what you enter or upload, diary records restored from your devices or older private iCloud archive, and information StrideBuddy and its AI providers derive from those inputs. If you turn on iPhone product analytics, the app also records limited workout and feature categories with a random analytics identifier to help us understand use and reliability. If you contact us, we use any health details you choose to include to respond to your message.

2. Who receives which data

Render stores your diary and results so we can sync them. Apple handles any older private iCloud archive you keep. OpenAI or Google Gemini can receive selected workouts and profile information to build a training week. Both can receive the request if the first attempt fails. Gemini handles changes to a week.

When we build or change a training week, we keep your saved notes and quick-note text out of the request to the AI provider. Session titles and results can still go to the provider. Race predictions also exclude your diary notes. Older versions that offer feedback on an individual workout can still send its notes to Gemini. OpenAI receives selected performance data for predictions. For AI logging, the provider receives the text, recording or photo you submit.

If you turn on product analytics, PostHog receives limited categories about workouts and app use, together with the analytics identifier. If you send us feedback or email, our delivery and mailbox providers handle what you include. Resend delivers in-app feedback. Its US provider TypeSafe checks a sample of outgoing emails for spam and phishing. Resend says TypeSafe neither stores the content nor uses it to train models. Apple and Google handle support mail. When you choose to share a workout, the app or recipient you choose can receive the content you select. If the law requires disclosure, we may provide the relevant records to the authority making the request. We have no affiliated companies that receive your consumer health data. We do not sell it.

3. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask whether we collect, share or sell consumer health data about you, access it, withdraw consent to its collection or sharing, or ask us to delete it. You can also ask for a list of the third parties and affiliates with whom we shared or sold your data and a way to contact those third parties.

Email support@stridebuddy.app and tell us which right you want to exercise. We may ask you to confirm your account. You do not need to send us your training notes to make a request. You can turn off AI and optional iPhone analytics separately in Settings and start account deletion there. If your request concerns analytics, we may need the random analytics identifier from your device to find the records. We notify the recipients required by law when you request deletion. You control any older private iCloud archive, personal backup or copy you have shared yourself.

If we decline a request, reply to our decision or email support@stridebuddy.app and ask us to review it. We will explain our decision on the appeal in writing within 45 days. You can also file a complaint with the Washington Attorney General.
