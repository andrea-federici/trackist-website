Washington State

Consumer health data privacy policy

Updated November 24, 2026

This policy applies to consumer health data under Washington’s My Health My Data Act. The act protects you if you live in Washington, and it also protects you if StrideBuddy collects your data in Washington. Andrea Federici, an independent developer based in Norway, runs StrideBuddy. In this policy, “we” means Andrea Federici. Our privacy policy explains how StrideBuddy handles all of your personal data.

1. What we collect and why

Some of what you log in StrideBuddy can reveal information about your health. For example, you can record how hard a session felt, choose a tag such as “Tired”, or write a note about pain or an injury. Your workouts, times and race results can also say something about your fitness.

StrideBuddy uses this information to store your training, to sync it across your devices and to show your progress. StrideBuddy also uses your athlete profile for the same purposes. Your athlete profile includes your events, your birth year, your competition category and your personal bests.

When you create an account, StrideBuddy asks whether you want to turn AI on. If you turn AI on, StrideBuddy uses selected training and profile information to suggest or change a training week and to estimate your performance. These estimates can include measures such as your VO₂max. When you ask StrideBuddy to log a workout from text, a photo or a recording, what you send can also contain health information. You can turn AI off in Settings at any time.

StrideBuddy gets this information from three sources. The first source is what you enter or upload. The second source is an older copy of your training that you restore from iCloud. The third source is what StrideBuddy and its AI providers work out from your information, such as race predictions.

If you turn on usage statistics in the iPhone app, StrideBuddy also records which kinds of workouts you log, together with a random ID. If you contact us, we use the health details you include only to answer your message.

2. Who receives your data

We do not sell your consumer health data, and no affiliated company receives it. These service providers receive it on our behalf:

  • Render stores your training data on our server so that your devices can sync.
  • OpenAI and Google Gemini receive selected training and profile information when you use the AI features. StrideBuddy does not send your notes when it builds or changes a training week, or when it predicts your race times. Session titles and results can still reach the provider. When you log a workout with AI, the provider receives the text, photo or recording that you send.
  • PostHog receives the kinds of workouts you log, if you turn on usage statistics in the iPhone app.
  • Resend and our email provider receive any health details that you include in feedback or in an email to us.
  • Apple keeps the older copy of your training in your iCloud, if you used StrideBuddy before accounts existed.

If the law requires us to disclose records, we may give the relevant records to the authority that asks for them.

3. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask us whether we collect, share or sell consumer health data about you. You can also ask to see that data, withdraw your consent to its collection or sharing, or ask us to delete it. You can ask for a list of the third parties and affiliates that received your data, together with a way to contact each of them.

To use any of these rights, email support@stridebuddy.app and say which right you want to use. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours, but you do not need to send us your training notes. You can also turn off AI and usage statistics in Settings, and you can delete your account there. Your usage statistics are not linked to your account. To find your usage statistics, we need the random ID that the app keeps on your iPhone. The email that opens when you tap “Request analytics deletion” in Settings includes this ID.

If we decline your request, you can appeal. To appeal, reply to our decision, or email support@stridebuddy.app and ask us to review it. If we decline your appeal, you can file a complaint with the Washington Attorney General.
