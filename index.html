Race predictions

StrideBuddy predicts your race times.

It reads your diary and predicts your main event and related distances. An 800m runner also sees the 400m and the 1500m.

Each prediction shows a likely time and a range.

A confidence level shows how sure it is.

A chart shows the last 6 months next to your PB.

Predictions start after you log 5 sessions.

A prediction is an estimate, not a promise. Predictions use AI, are free with usage limits, and need an internet connection.

Volume

See how much you ran each week.

A dashed line shows your usual week.

Records

Your best reps and races are on one page.

Records shows your personal bests and your fastest rep at each distance.

You can add the PBs you already have.

A faster race in your diary updates your PB.

You can see one season or all time.

Hurdle reps have their own records.

Races

All your upcoming races are in one calendar.

Add a race and choose how important it is. Plan uses your races to shape your training.

Each race shows how many days are left.

An A race gets a full taper, a B race gets a short taper, and you train through a C race.

A logged race counts toward your personal bests.
