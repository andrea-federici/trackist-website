Coach

Your training week, built for your season.

Coach builds a weekly plan from your event, recent training, available days and races. The season sets the direction. Your diary helps it choose how much work to prescribe.

Season statusRead where you are in the season and what your training is working toward.
This weekUnderstand the focus of the week before you open its sessions.
Your event and racesTraining reflects your event and the importance of the races in your calendar.
You choose when to buildAsk for your week when you are ready. Saved plans remain available offline.

Coach uses AI to write the plan within event-specific training rules. It is a free beta feature and needs an internet connection to build or change a plan.

Modern periodization

Change the focus as your season progresses.

Periodization means changing the focus of training over time. Build a base, develop the qualities your event needs, then prepare for racing. Easier weeks and recovery are part of the plan.

Coach keeps useful training qualities in the plan as the main focus changes. It considers your recent sessions and race priorities when choosing the next week's work.

A return from a break starts with a gradual return to training. A nearby race can change the week's preparation. The plan follows your situation.

Records

Your reps and races, side by side.

Records brings your fastest reps and races onto one page. A hand-drawn signature curve follows the distances you run, and the ledger lists your times by distance. You can look at a single season or your full history.

Your speed curveSee your rep speed at every distance, with race results overlaid.
Times by distanceCompare your fastest and typical times for reps or races.
One season or all timeFocus on one season or open your complete training history.
Times without judgmentA slower rep isn’t treated as a bad rep. It’s simply part of the record.

Races

The races you have coming, and the ones you have run.

The race calendar keeps upcoming meets and past results on one page. A race you entered weeks ahead and a race you logged straight into the diary both appear here, so the list stays complete.
