Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Trackist respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain language, what the app stores and why.

What Trackist stores

Trackist stores the workout and athlete data you enter, including sessions, reps, splits, recoveries, notes, race results, personal bests, and your athlete profile. This data powers training logging, your diary, and personal-best tracking.

How it’s used

Your data is used to power the features of the app: logging sessions, organizing your diary, categorizing workouts around your event, and tracking personal bests. It is not sold.
