Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

StrideBuddy respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain language, what the app keeps on your device, what is sent to StrideBuddy’s backend when you use AI-powered features, and what limited technical data is used to keep the service reliable and safe. It should be read together with the StrideBuddy Terms of Use.

StrideBuddy is for adults 18 and older. It is not directed to children or teens.

Local-first workout data

Your training diary is stored on your device. StrideBuddy’s database does not store your raw workout diary, athlete profile, race results, personal bests, notes, reps, splits, recoveries, or strength entries as a permanent user record.

The app uses the data you enter on device to power logging, diary views, workout categorization, personal-best tracking, Daily Review, and future AI features.

Information StrideBuddy processes

StrideBuddy may process the following categories of information, depending on how you use the app:

  • training diary content, such as workouts, notes, RPE, feeling, tags, races, personal bests, records, and generated review output stored on your device;
  • profile and event context, such as competition category, event, athlete type, timezone, and event-specific personal-best context;
  • AI request data, only when AI features are enabled and a request is needed;
  • technical and security data, such as pseudonymous install identifiers, request identifiers, source hashes, rate-limit counters, salted IP hashes, app version, locale, and policy-acceptance metadata; and
  • support communications, such as your email address and message content if you contact StrideBuddy for help, feedback, privacy requests, or deletion requests.

AI features

After you confirm you are at least 18 and accept StrideBuddy’s terms, privacy policy, and AI disclosure, AI-powered features may run automatically. When an AI feature runs, StrideBuddy sends only the information needed to generate that feature to StrideBuddy’s backend, which then sends the relevant prompt data to Google’s paid Gemini API to produce the response.

Depending on the feature, this may include:

  • a pseudonymous app-install identifier and a one-time request identifier;
  • your local date, timezone, and a source hash used to detect whether the underlying diary input changed;
  • your event profile, such as event distance, sex, and personal-best times;
  • the workout being reviewed and compact recent workout summaries, including dates, workout type, title, compact summary, category, RPE, feeling, notes, and compact facts such as distance, duration, race time, exercise names, or obstacle-work details;
  • a compact on-device summary of recent prior weeks, such as session counts, category counts, recent race logs, repeated body-signal terms from notes, average RPE, and low-feeling counts; and
  • for Daily Review, compact prior Daily Review context when it helps connect a newly logged workout to recent training.

Because notes are written by you, they may include health-related or other sensitive details if you choose to enter them. Do not include information in notes that you do not want processed for AI-powered features.

StrideBuddy does not send your full historical diary to Gemini. Recent history is reduced on device into a small summary before it is sent. StrideBuddy uses the paid Google Gemini API, and Google states that data submitted to paid Gemini API services is not used to train Google’s AI models. Google may process Gemini API inputs and outputs as described in Google’s applicable terms and privacy commitments, including the Gemini API Additional Terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

AI disclosure

Daily Review and future AI features generate text from selected diary data. AI output may be inaccurate or incomplete. StrideBuddy is not medical advice, a diagnosis, injury-risk scoring, emergency support, or a guaranteed training plan. Do not rely on StrideBuddy as your only source for training, health, injury, or safety decisions.

AI features are optional after onboarding through one master AI features toggle in the You tab. Turning AI off stops future AI requests. Local logging and diary viewing remain available.

Backend and security data

StrideBuddy’s backend uses limited technical data to operate AI features, enforce generation limits, prevent abuse, and protect the service from malicious traffic. This can include:

  • pseudonymous app-install identifiers;
  • request identifiers and source hashes;
  • timezone information used to validate review windows;
  • rate-limit counters for AI requests;
  • minimal AI terms acceptance metadata, such as accepted policy versions, app version, locale or region when available, adult confirmation, and enabled state; and
  • a salted hash of the request IP address. StrideBuddy uses the IP address received with a request to create this hash for abuse prevention; the backend rate-limit records store the hash, not the raw IP address.

Daily Review output is stored on your device. StrideBuddy’s backend may keep limited technical records described above to operate AI features, enforce generation limits, prevent abuse, and debug reliability. The durable copy of AI-generated content is stored on your device.

How StrideBuddy uses information

StrideBuddy uses information to:

  • provide local logging, diary views, workout categorization, records, and app settings;
  • generate Daily Review and future AI features when AI is enabled;
  • remember Terms, Privacy Policy, and AI disclosure acceptance status;
  • enforce request caps, prevent abuse, debug reliability issues, and protect the backend;
  • respond to support, privacy, deletion, or legal requests; and
  • comply with laws, enforce the Terms, and protect rights, safety, and security.

Retention

Your raw diary data remains on your device unless selected data is needed for an enabled AI feature. Backend rate-limit records are kept for short operational windows, generally about 24 hours. StrideBuddy may retain limited operational records for longer when needed to protect the service, investigate abuse, comply with law, or resolve security issues.

Sharing

StrideBuddy does not sell your personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. StrideBuddy shares AI request data with Google Gemini only to provide the AI-powered features, and may use infrastructure providers that host the backend, caching, networking, or security systems needed to run the service. Service providers are expected to protect the data they process for StrideBuddy and use it only for the services they provide to StrideBuddy, unless you separately agree or the law requires otherwise.

StrideBuddy may also disclose limited information when required by law, to enforce the Terms, to protect users or the service, or as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar business transfer. If a business transfer occurs, StrideBuddy will require the recipient to handle personal information consistently with this policy unless you are given notice and any choices required by law.

Your control

You control the diary data stored on your device through the app. AI-powered processing is part of StrideBuddy’s core experience: when you accept StrideBuddy’s terms, privacy policy, and AI disclosure during onboarding or an update flow, you agree that the selected diary data needed for Daily Review and related AI features may be processed by StrideBuddy’s backend and Google Gemini as described above. The app includes one master AI features toggle in the You tab. Turning it off stops future AI requests, while local logging and diary viewing remain available.

Depending on where you live, you may have privacy rights such as access, correction, deletion, or objection rights. Because StrideBuddy is local-first and does not maintain user accounts, some requests may require information that lets us locate the relevant backend record, such as an app-install identifier or request details.

Deletion and requests

You can delete local diary data by deleting entries in the app or deleting the app from your device, subject to any iOS backups or exports you control. You can request deletion of backend AI metadata, cache records, or support messages by contacting StrideBuddy. Because StrideBuddy has no account system, StrideBuddy may need request details, timestamps, app-install information, or other information from you to locate the relevant backend records.

StrideBuddy will respond to privacy requests within the time required by applicable law. Some information may be retained when needed for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or where deletion is not technically possible from backups until they expire.

European, UK, and Swiss privacy rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, privacy laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, and Swiss data-protection law may give you rights over personal data. StrideBuddy processes data to provide the app and AI features you request, based on contract, consent, legitimate interests such as security and abuse prevention, and legal obligations where applicable.

You may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a portable copy of personal data. You may also be able to withdraw consent for future processing. Turning off the master AI features toggle stops future AI requests; you can also contact StrideBuddy for privacy requests. Withdrawing consent does not make earlier lawful processing invalid.

Because diary notes are written by you, they may include health-related or other sensitive details if you choose to enter them. Do not include sensitive information in StrideBuddy that you do not want processed for enabled app features.

You may have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. In Italy, that authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali; in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office; and in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

California and US state privacy rights

StrideBuddy does not sell personal information, share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, use targeted advertising, include analytics SDKs, or track users across third-party apps or websites. Because StrideBuddy has no sale, sharing, targeted-advertising, or cross-site tracking flow, browser Do Not Track signals and Global Privacy Control signals do not change StrideBuddy’s behavior.

Where California or other US state privacy laws apply, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, a copy of personal information, or information about certain disclosures. StrideBuddy will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights, subject to legal limits and the local-first/no-account design described above.

International transfers

StrideBuddy, Google, and infrastructure providers may process data in countries other than where you live. When required, StrideBuddy relies on appropriate safeguards for international transfers, such as contractual protections provided by the relevant service providers.

Security

StrideBuddy uses a local-first design, limited request payloads, pseudonymous identifiers, request caps, and backend logging rules intended to reduce privacy risk. No app or internet service can guarantee perfect security.

Children and teens

StrideBuddy is for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children or teens. Do not use StrideBuddy if you are under 18. If you believe a child or teen has sent personal information to StrideBuddy’s backend, contact us so we can review and delete the relevant backend records where possible.

Changes

StrideBuddy may update this policy as the app, providers, or legal requirements change. If a change materially affects AI data use, provider, purpose, or risk, StrideBuddy will require renewed in-app acceptance before AI features run again.

Questions

If you have questions about privacy, contact: support@stridebuddy.app
