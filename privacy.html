Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

This policy explains what StrideBuddy keeps on your device, what the app sends to StrideBuddy’s backend when AI features run, and the limited technical data used to keep the service working and secure. Please read it together with the StrideBuddy Terms of Use.

StrideBuddy is intended for adults aged 18 or older, not for children or teenagers.

Local-first workout data

Your training diary stays on your device. StrideBuddy’s database does not keep your raw workout diary, athlete profile, race results, personal bests, notes, reps, splits, recoveries, or strength entries as a permanent user record.

StrideBuddy uses the data you enter on your device for logging, diary views, workout categorization, personal-best tracking, Daily Review, and future AI features.

Information StrideBuddy processes

The information StrideBuddy processes depends on how you use the app and may include:

  • training diary content stored on your device, including workouts, notes, RPE, feeling, tags, races, personal bests, records, and generated review output;
  • profile and event details, including your competition category, event, athlete type, timezone, and event-specific personal-best context;
  • data required for an AI request, but only when AI features are enabled and need to make a request;
  • technical and security information, including pseudonymous install identifiers, request identifiers, source hashes, rate-limit counters, salted IP hashes, app version, locale, and policy-acceptance metadata; and
  • messages you send to StrideBuddy for support, feedback, privacy requests, or deletion requests, along with details such as your email address.

AI features

AI features may run automatically after you confirm that you are at least 18 and accept StrideBuddy’s terms, privacy policy, and AI disclosure. When a feature runs, the app sends only the information that feature needs to StrideBuddy’s backend. The backend then sends the relevant prompt data to Google’s paid Gemini API to generate a response.

Depending on which feature runs, the request may contain:

  • a pseudonymous app-install identifier and a request identifier used only once;
  • your local date and timezone, plus a source hash that checks whether the underlying diary input has changed;
  • your event profile, which may include event distance, sex, and personal-best times;
  • the workout under review and brief summaries of recent workouts, including dates, workout type, title, summary, category, RPE, feeling, notes, and facts such as distance, duration, race time, exercise names, or obstacle-work details;
  • a brief summary created on your device from recent weeks, which may include session and category counts, recent race logs, repeated body-signal terms from notes, average RPE, and low-feeling counts; and
  • for Daily Review, brief context from earlier Daily Reviews when it helps relate a new workout to your recent training.

You write your own notes, so they may contain health information or other sensitive details if you choose to add them. Do not put anything in your notes that you do not want AI features to process.

StrideBuddy does not send your complete diary history to Gemini. Before anything is sent, the app reduces recent history to a short summary on your device. StrideBuddy uses the paid Google Gemini API. Google states that it does not use data submitted to its paid Gemini API services to train Google AI models. Google may process Gemini API input and output under its applicable terms and privacy commitments, including the Gemini API Additional Terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

AI disclosure

Daily Review and future AI features use selected diary data to generate text. That text may be wrong or incomplete. StrideBuddy does not provide medical advice, diagnoses, injury-risk scores, emergency support, or a guaranteed training plan. It should not be your only source for decisions about training, health, injuries, or safety.

After onboarding, you can control every AI feature with one master toggle in the You tab. Turning it off prevents future AI requests. You can still log workouts and view your diary on your device.

Backend and security data

StrideBuddy’s backend uses a limited amount of technical data to run AI features, apply generation limits, prevent abuse, and block malicious traffic. That data may include:

  • pseudonymous app-install identifiers;
  • request identifiers and source hashes;
  • timezone information for checking review windows;
  • counters that enforce limits on AI requests;
  • limited metadata about your acceptance of AI terms, including accepted policy versions, app version, locale or region when available, adult confirmation, and whether AI is enabled; and
  • a salted hash created from the request’s IP address for abuse prevention. Backend rate-limit records keep the hash rather than the raw IP address.

Daily Review output stays on your device. The backend may keep the limited technical records described above to run AI features, apply generation limits, prevent abuse, and investigate reliability problems. Your device holds the lasting copy of AI-generated content.

How StrideBuddy uses information

StrideBuddy processes information for the following purposes:

  • to provide on-device logging, diary views, workout categorization, records, and app settings;
  • to generate Daily Review and future AI features when AI is enabled;
  • to remember whether you accepted the Terms, Privacy Policy, and AI disclosure;
  • to apply request limits, prevent abuse, investigate reliability problems, and protect the backend;
  • to answer support, privacy, deletion, or legal requests; and
  • to comply with the law, enforce the Terms, and protect rights, safety, and security.

Retention

Your raw diary data stays on your device unless an enabled AI feature needs selected data. The backend usually keeps rate-limit records for about 24 hours. StrideBuddy may keep limited operational records longer when necessary to protect the service, investigate abuse, comply with the law, or resolve security problems.

Sharing

StrideBuddy does not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. AI request data goes to Google Gemini only to provide AI features. StrideBuddy may also use infrastructure providers for the hosting, caching, networking, or security systems needed to run the service. These providers are expected to protect the data they process for StrideBuddy and use it only to provide their services, unless you agree separately or the law requires another use.

StrideBuddy may disclose limited information when the law requires it, to enforce the Terms, to protect users or the service, or during a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, asset sale, or similar business transfer. In a business transfer, StrideBuddy will require the recipient to handle personal information in line with this policy unless you receive notice and any choices required by law.

Your control

You control the diary data on your device through the app. AI processing is part of StrideBuddy’s core experience. When you accept the terms, privacy policy, and AI disclosure during onboarding or an update, you agree that StrideBuddy’s backend and Google Gemini may process the selected diary data needed for Daily Review and related AI features as described above. A master toggle in the You tab controls all AI features. Turning it off stops future AI requests but leaves local logging and diary viewing available.

Your location may give you privacy rights, including rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your data. StrideBuddy is local-first and does not use accounts, so we may need an app-install identifier, request details, or similar information to find a relevant backend record.

Deletion and requests

Delete diary entries in the app to remove them locally, or delete the app to remove its local data from your device. Any iOS backups or exports you control may still contain copies. To request deletion of backend AI metadata, cache records, or support messages, contact StrideBuddy. Because there are no user accounts, we may need request details, timestamps, app-install information, or other details to find the relevant backend records.

StrideBuddy will answer privacy requests within the period required by applicable law. Some information may be kept when necessary for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution. Information in backups may also remain until deletion becomes technically possible or those backups expire.

European, UK, and Swiss privacy rights

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, or Swiss data-protection law may give you rights over your personal data. Where applicable, StrideBuddy processes data to provide the app and requested AI features based on contract, consent, legitimate interests such as security and abuse prevention, and legal obligations.

You may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a portable copy of your personal data. You may also be able to withdraw consent for future processing. You can stop future AI requests by turning off the master AI features toggle, and you can contact StrideBuddy with privacy requests. Withdrawing consent does not invalidate processing that was lawful when it occurred.

Your diary notes may contain health information or other sensitive details if you choose to write them. Do not add sensitive information that you do not want enabled app features to process.

You may be entitled to complain to your local data protection authority. In Italy, this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. In the UK, it is the Information Commissioner’s Office. In Switzerland, it is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

California and US state privacy rights

StrideBuddy does not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, use targeted advertising, include analytics SDKs, or track people across third-party apps or websites. Because none of these activities occur, browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals do not change how StrideBuddy behaves.

If California or another US state privacy law applies to you, you may have the right to request access, correction, deletion, a copy of your personal information, or details about certain disclosures. Subject to legal limits and the local-first design without accounts described above, StrideBuddy will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

International transfers

StrideBuddy, Google, and infrastructure providers may process data outside the country where you live. When the law requires it, StrideBuddy relies on appropriate transfer safeguards, including contractual protections from the relevant service providers.

Security

To reduce privacy risk, StrideBuddy keeps your diary local, limits request data, uses pseudonymous identifiers and request caps, and restricts backend logging. No app or online service can promise complete security.

Children and teens

StrideBuddy is intended for adults aged 18 or older, not for children or teenagers. Do not use the app if you are under 18. If you believe a child or teenager sent personal information to StrideBuddy’s backend, contact us. We will review the request and delete relevant backend records where possible.

Changes

StrideBuddy may revise this policy when the app, its providers, or legal requirements change. If a revision materially changes the data, provider, purpose, or risk involved in AI processing, AI features will not run again until you accept the new policy in the app.

Questions
