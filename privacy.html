Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the StrideBuddy iPhone app, the web app at web.stridebuddy.app and this website. The policy explains what personal data StrideBuddy uses, why we use it, who receives it and what you can control. Our terms of use set out the rules for using StrideBuddy, and we recommend that you read them together with this policy.

Andrea Federici, an independent developer based in Norway, runs StrideBuddy and is responsible for your personal data. In this policy, “we” means Andrea Federici. You can contact us by email at support@stridebuddy.app or by post at Nygårdvegen 2, 9019 Tromsø, Norway.

These are the most important points:

  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Neither we nor our AI providers use your data to train AI models.
  • You can turn AI on or off in Settings at any time, and StrideBuddy keeps working when AI is off.
  • The iPhone app shares usage statistics only if you agree.
  • You can delete your account and your training data in the app at any time.

1. Your account and training data

You need an account to use StrideBuddy, and you sign in with your Apple or Google account. Firebase Authentication, a Google service, manages the sign-in. When you sign in, StrideBuddy receives your email address. StrideBuddy also receives your name if you choose to share it. Firebase also provides the identifiers that keep you signed in.

StrideBuddy stores what you add to the app, such as your athlete profile, the workouts and races you log, your personal bests, your notes and your plans. Your athlete profile includes your birth year and your competition category. StrideBuddy also stores your settings, the terms you accepted and the list of devices that use your account.

The iPhone app and the web app keep a copy of your training data on your device so that they can work offline. When you sign out, the app removes this copy from your device.

2. AI features

StrideBuddy uses AI to suggest a training week, to change a week, to estimate your performance and to log a workout from text, a photo or a recording. When you create an account, StrideBuddy asks whether you want to turn AI on. You can change this choice in Settings at any time, and every other feature stays available when AI is off. For each feature, StrideBuddy sends the AI provider only the information that the feature needs:

  • To suggest or change a training week, StrideBuddy sends OpenAI or Google Gemini your events, your age group and your personal bests. StrideBuddy also sends your availability, your upcoming races and the training you logged in the last five weeks. Your notes are not sent.
  • To predict your race times and to estimate measures such as your VO₂max and your running qualities, StrideBuddy sends OpenAI your events, your competition category and your personal bests. StrideBuddy also sends your races from the last year and your training from the last six months, including the titles you gave your workouts. Your notes are not sent. StrideBuddy updates these estimates automatically when you log new training.
  • To log a workout for you, StrideBuddy sends the text you type to OpenAI and the photo or recording you choose to Google Gemini. Before the iPhone sends a photo, the iPhone removes the photo’s location. You check the entry before StrideBuddy saves it.

When you turn AI off, StrideBuddy stops sending new requests. StrideBuddy cannot recall information that a provider has already received. OpenAI and Google may keep requests for a limited time to detect abuse. The pages of OpenAI and Google explain how long each company keeps requests.

3. Usage statistics

The iPhone app asks whether you want to share usage statistics. If you agree, PostHog receives information about how you use the app, such as which features you open, which kinds of workouts you log and whether syncing works. Each event carries a random ID that stays on your iPhone and is not linked to your account. The events never include your workout details, your notes or your results. PostHog is set up to discard your IP address. The web app does not collect usage statistics. You can change your choice in Settings at any time.

4. Support and feedback

When you email us or send feedback from the app, we receive your email address and your message. Feedback sent from the app also includes your account ID and some details about your device and your app version. The Resend service delivers this feedback to us by email. In your message, please include only the details we need to help you.

5. Our website

Cloudflare hosts our website. When you visit the website, Cloudflare receives your IP address and the addresses of the pages on our website that you open. The website uses no cookies for analytics or advertising.

6. Why we use your data

In the European Economic Area and the UK, the law requires a legal basis for each use of personal data. These are our legal bases:

  • To provide StrideBuddy, including your account, your training data and the AI features you use, our legal basis is our contract with you.
  • To understand how people use the iPhone app, our legal basis is your consent.
  • To keep accounts secure, prevent abuse, fix errors and answer support requests, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a safe service that works well.
  • To meet our legal obligations, such as answering a privacy request, our legal basis is the law itself. To defend a legal claim, or if StrideBuddy passes to a new owner, we may share the necessary data within the limits that the law allows.

Some of what you log can reveal information about your health, such as how you felt or a note about an injury. StrideBuddy uses this information only to provide its features to you. Where the law requires your consent for AI features, or your explicit consent for health information, we follow that requirement. You can leave such details out, delete them or ask us to remove them. Our consumer health data privacy policy explains this topic in more detail.

7. Who processes your data and where

  • Render runs our server and our database in Frankfurt, Germany, and serves the web app. Render also processes some data in the United States.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in, and Firebase Authentication processes sign-in data in the United States.
  • OpenAI and Google provide the AI features and process data in the United States and in other countries.
  • PostHog stores the iPhone app’s usage statistics in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Resend delivers feedback sent from the app through Ireland and stores the emails in the United States.
  • Our email provider stores the support emails that you send us.
  • Cloudflare hosts our website.

When a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, we rely on one of the safeguards that the GDPR allows, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. You can write to us to ask for more details or for a copy of these safeguards.

8. How long we keep data

We keep your training data and your plans for as long as you have an account. When you delete an entry, the entry disappears from the app. Older copies of the entry can remain in the records that keep your devices in sync and in the history of your plans. These records have no fixed deletion date. Deleted data also stays in our backups until the backups are replaced automatically. Most security and usage-limit records last a day or a week, and some installation records last longer.

We remove feedback from our database 30 days after it arrives, and we keep support emails for as long as we need them to help you and to follow up. Resend keeps its own copy of the feedback for 30 days. You can ask us at any time to delete your support emails. PostHog keeps usage statistics for 12 months.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in Settings. When you delete your account, we delete your training data, your plans, your estimates and your sync records from our server, and we ask Firebase to delete your sign-in data. Google says that it removes this data within 180 days. Our server keeps only a short record of the deletion, with your account identifiers and the dates, so that an old device cannot restore the account. If you delete the app from your phone, your account still exists.

Deleting your account does not delete your support emails or your usage statistics. You can ask us to delete your support emails. Your usage statistics are not linked to your account, so we can find them only with the random ID that the app keeps on your iPhone. To have your usage statistics deleted, tap “Request analytics deletion” in Settings before you remove the app. The email that opens contains this ID. If you used StrideBuddy before accounts existed, you can delete the old copy of your data in your iCloud settings.

10. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, and you can ask us to correct or delete your data. You can also ask us to limit how we use your data, object to how we use it, or give it to you in a format that you can use elsewhere. Where our legal basis is your consent, you can withdraw your consent at any time. To make any of these requests, write to support@stridebuddy.app. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours, but we will never ask for your password. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, or to the data protection authority in the country where you live.

In the United States, your privacy rights depend on the state where you live. You can use the same email address to make a request or to appeal one of our decisions. We will not treat you differently because you use your rights. Our consumer health data privacy policy explains which rights you have over your health data.

11. Age and changes to this policy

StrideBuddy is for people aged 18 and over. If you think that someone younger has given us personal data, please contact us, and we will delete that data. When we change this policy, we update the date at the top of the page. StrideBuddy tells you about important changes in the app, and StrideBuddy asks for your permission first when a change needs it.
