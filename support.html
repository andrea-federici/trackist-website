Account and diary help

StrideBuddy requires an account. Existing users must accept the current Terms and Privacy Policy, sign in, and complete a verified migration before using the server-storage version. After migration, use Settings to check diary sync, export your diary, sign out, or delete your account.

If an existing diary does not match during migration, StrideBuddy stops before making the server copy the main copy. Your diary remains saved on the device and in private iCloud, but the app stays at the retry and support screen until migration succeeds. Do not delete the app or its iCloud data. Contact support with the safe diagnostic code shown in the app. Do not email workout notes, account tokens, or private keys.

Support cannot move a diary to another account based only on an email address. You may need to sign in again with the same Apple Account that owns the StrideBuddy account.

Delete or export your data

Use the on-device export before deletion if you want to keep a copy. Account deletion starts at the bottom of Settings and requires a recent Sign in with Apple confirmation. It removes the StrideBuddy server account, diary, and operational backend records linked to its registered installations.

An older private iCloud archive, iOS backup, or export is not removed automatically. You control those copies through Apple’s settings and the files you saved.
