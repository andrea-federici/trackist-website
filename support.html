Account help

You need an account to use StrideBuddy, and you sign in with Apple or Google. Signing in with Apple and signing in with Google create two separate accounts. Always sign in the same way you did when you created your account.

If you used StrideBuddy before accounts existed, the app moves your workouts to your account the first time you sign in. If this step fails, the app keeps your workouts on your iPhone and shows a Contact support button. Tap the button to send us an email that includes the error details. Please don’t delete the app until we have solved the problem together.

We cannot move your training data to a different account just because you ask us by email. If you can’t sign in, write to us. Tell us whether you created your account with Apple or with Google, and which email address that Apple or Google account uses.

Delete your account

You can delete your account in Settings, in the iPhone app or in the web app. Before StrideBuddy deletes your account, you sign in again with the same Apple or Google account. StrideBuddy then deletes your training data, your plans and your race predictions from our server.

Deleting your account does not delete your support emails or your usage statistics. You can ask us by email to delete your support emails. To have your usage statistics deleted, tap “Request analytics deletion” in Settings before you remove the app.
